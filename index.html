About the Educator

A Journey Rooted in the Quran

Rauf Sajjad is a certified Quran teacher with 5+ years of teaching experience and 100+ students taught across USA, UK, and Gulf countries. He began his Quranic path at an early age, completing memorization of the Holy Quran (Hifz) under rigorous scholarly supervision and receiving formal Ijazah certification with connected Sanad from Al Quran Academy.

Over the years, he has merged traditional pedagogical rigor with contemporary empathetic mentorship. He believes every student approaches the Quran with distinct natural rhythms, verbal cadence, and spiritual motivations — requiring not rigid conformity, but thoughtful nurturing, clear phonetic breakdowns, and consistent encouragement.
